## Revision policy

Released PDFs are fixed editions. Later substantive changes receive a new version and a reason: correction, changed assumption, revised claim, source comparison or experimental result. Editorial changes must not silently strengthen or weaken a claim. An unconfirmed or rejected claim remains visible in the history. The living canonical page remains a single entry point.
