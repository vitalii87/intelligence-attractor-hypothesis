## Description to paste

The Intelligence Attractor Hypothesis (IAH) proposes that independently formed intelligent systems progressively lose origin-contingent functional differences as they approach a shared performance frontier under a fixed task, objective, context and admissible design space. Weak IAH predicts functional narrowing and origin attenuation; Strong IAH conjectures convergence to one functional equivalence class at a specified level. Architecture is a central level of application when it is mutable and performance-relevant.

This conceptual preprint includes the canonical statement and five appendices on origin dependence, recursive architectural optimization, relational narrowing and functional uniqueness, preliminary related work, and speculative limits including absolute architectural uniqueness. It reports no empirical validation. The initial intuition was developed by the author before acquaintance with adjacent literature; AI assistants contributed to formulation, criticism, editing and source discovery. The source map is preliminary, and neither novelty nor priority is established. References to overlooked work, counterexamples and corrections are welcome. Substantive revisions will be versioned.
